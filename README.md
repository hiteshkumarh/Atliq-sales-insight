# 🧩 AtliQ Hardwares: Sales Insight and Performance Dashboard

### 📊 Power BI Dashboard | 📑 Report | 🎯 Sales Insights



---

## 1. Introduction & Executive Summary

This project focuses on developing a **Sales Analytics and Business Intelligence solution** for **AtliQ Hardwares**, a company in the **FMCG sector** specializing in **computer hardware products**.

The company was facing challenges in **scaling revenue** in a competitive market due to the **absence of a unified, data-driven performance monitoring system**.

To address this, the **Sales Director** initiated a BI project to build an **interactive Power BI dashboard** that provides:

* Real-time visibility into sales performance
* Regional and product-level insights
* Actionable intelligence for strategic decision-making

---

## 2. Problem Statement

AtliQ Hardwares needed to overcome challenges in tracking and optimizing sales across multiple regions.
The Sales Director required a **data-driven system** capable of providing **real-time, actionable insights** to improve revenue performance and operational efficiency.

---

## 3. Project Objectives

The primary objective was to build a **comprehensive Sales Intelligence Dashboard** to:

* Develop an **interactive Power BI dashboard** for real-time sales insights
* Monitor **sales quantity, revenue, and performance** across markets
* Identify **top-performing products and customers** driving revenue
* Detect **declining revenue trends** and areas needing attention
* Empower decision-makers with **actionable insights** to enhance revenue growth

## 6. Solution Approach

A structured data analytics workflow was followed:

1. **Data Preparation:**

   * Imported raw sales data into MySQL
   * Performed data cleaning and preprocessing

2. **Metric Calculation:**

   * Used SQL queries to calculate metrics like Revenue, Sales Quantity, and YoY trends

3. **Data Visualization:**

   * Connected processed data from MySQL to Power BI
   * Created visuals for regional and product-level performance

4. **Dashboard Design:**

   * Added dynamic filters for Year, Region, and Product
   * Designed intuitive visuals and KPIs for interactive exploration

---

## 7. Dashboard & Key Visuals

The **Power BI Dashboard** provides a centralized, interactive view of company-wide sales performance.

### 🔑 Key Features:

* **Revenue by Market:** Comparison of total sales across major cities
* **Sales Quantity by Market:** Regional demand analysis
* **Revenue Trend Over Time:** Year-over-Year trend visualization
* **Top 5 Customers & Products:** Key revenue drivers
* **Dynamic Slicers & Filters:** Interactive analysis by Year, Month, and Region

---

## 8. Key Insights

* **Top Region:** Delhi generated the highest revenue (₹519M)
* **Revenue Trend:** Gradual decline since 2019 indicating potential pricing or demand issues
* **Customer Dependency:** Top 5 customers contributed ~70% of total revenue
* **Growth Opportunities:** Mumbai and Ahmedabad show high growth potential
* **Product Performance:** Clear differentiation between high and low-performing products
